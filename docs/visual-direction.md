# Visual direction — Clarté Design

Primary reference: `token-design.jpeg`, provided by the owner. The owner explicitly requested closer adherence to this image after reviewing the initial implementation.

Preserve:
- Sand outer frame, ivory page, espresso text, caramel and taupe accents.
- Circular C monogram and tracked serif wordmark.
- Moderate serif headline, upright opening and italic emphasis in the same ink color.
- Flat front-facing browser and phone mockups as the hero's focal point.
- Hairline borders, pill buttons, rounded rectangular cards, generous whitespace.
- Flat landscape artwork: caramel sun, taupe hills, espresso foreground.
- Four numbered design principles placed below the mockups.

Avoid:
- Large three-line editorial hero that displaces the mockups.
- Glass blobs, metallic orbital rings, particles, grain overlays.
- Full-screen canvas behind copy, custom cursors, magnetic buttons and tilted cards.
- Heavy shadows, glassmorphic navigation or oversized dark footer.

Animation is an enhancement, not a new art direction. Use Three.js only inside the landscape illustration; slow sun/hill motion and very small pointer parallax. Respect reduced motion and pause rendering when the illustration is offscreen.

The image is a visual brand reference, not a specification with labelled hex codes or font names. Current tokens are approximations; do not claim exact extraction. Mockup Aura is illustrative, not a verified client project.
